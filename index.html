Veridion · Regulatory Intelligence

RegMatrix

Source-backed, state-by-state cannabis compliance intelligence — built as a coordinated set of documents that together map a state's regulatory landscape, its gray zones, field operations, statutory authority, policy rationale, and competitive context.

The RegMatrix Doctrine

Every complete state offering is composed of six documents. Each stands alone; together they are the product.

Entity

State Profile

Regulatory snapshot — agencies, legal status, license types, METRC, key statutes, tax architecture.

Gray Zones

Penumbrant Paper

Seven penumbral risk zones — where the rule is unclear, contested, or untested.

Field Ops

WCE

Operational reference — METRC mechanics, inspection checklists, transport SOPs, compliance calendar, enforcement playbooks.

Authority

RegBible

Statutory authority — citations per compliance domain, organized for quick lookup.

Policy

Reg & Pol

Analytical layer — legislative history, stakeholder map, current debates, policy tensions.

Context

Context Map

Regulatory context — timeline, economics, enforcement climate, cross-state comparison.

Methodology

Two boundaries define the quality of an offering.

WCE vs. Reg & Pol. The WCE is the mechanic — what an operator does, and by when. Reg & Pol is the why and who — how the rules got that way and who is still fighting over them. A procedure that needs a gray-zone caveat gets one sentence of reference, not three paragraphs of legislative intent.

Source-backed, confidence-tagged. Every claim carries a citation and a high / medium / low confidence tag. Sources are point-in-time; stale content is flagged. This is regulatory intelligence, not legal advice.
