# Minnesota RegBible — Statutory Authority Reference

## Overview

Statutory authority for each compliance domain in Minnesota's cannabis framework. Minnesota's legal hierarchy runs: Minnesota Constitution → Minn. Stat. Chapter 390 (adult-use) → Minn. Stat. Chapter 152 (medical) → OCM/DOH administrative rules → local ordinances. This document organizes statutory authority by compliance domain. Where rules are in permanent rulemaking (Minn. R. Ch. 8200–8215), the current emergency rule authority is noted — permanent rules may differ.

---

## Compliance Domains

### 1. Licensing

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| OCM licensing authority | Minn. Stat. §390.05 | All adult-use license categories |
| License types established | Minn. Stat. §390.06 | CULT, MFG, RET, WHLS, TEST, TRAN, EVT, MICRO |
| Social equity licensing | Minn. Stat. §390.07 | 50%+ of licenses reserved for qualifying applicants |
| License application requirements | Minn. Stat. §390.05; OCM guidance | Background checks, financial disclosures, premises requirements |
| License renewal | Minn. Stat. §390.05 | Annual renewal; OCM schedule |
| Conditional licenses | OCM emergency rules | Available before full compliance established |
| Local authority — zoning | Minn. Stat. §390.09 | Municipalities may require local license in addition to state license |
| Medical dispensary licensing (DOH) | Minn. Stat. §152.22 | Separate from OCM adult-use licensing |
| Medical dispensary expansion | HF 100 (2023) | Expanded medical dispensary cap |

### 2. Operations

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Canopy tier structure | Minn. Stat. §390.06 | Tiers 1–5; square footage limits per tier |
| Cultivation environmental controls | OCM emergency rules | Environmental standards for indoor/outdoor/hybrid |
| Pesticide restrictions | OCM rules; MDA Chapter 1505 | OCM-approved pesticide list; MDA hazardous materials rules apply |
| Extraction methods — approved | OCM guidance | Butane, CO2, ethanol, solventless pre-approved; novel methods require pre-approval |
| Extraction methods — prohibited | OCM rules | No dangerous extraction methods without OCM approval |
| Manufacturing ingredient restrictions | OCM rules | No prohibited additives; no toxic excipients |
| Batch testing requirements | Minn. Stat. §390.14 | All product tested before sale/transfer |
| Testing laboratory standards | OCM rules | ISO 17025; proficiency testing; no financial interest in licensees |
| Waste management | OCM rules | Documented destruction; witness requirement |
| Security requirements | OCM rules | Alarm systems; camera coverage; secure storage |
| Visitor logs | OCM rules | All facility visitors documented |

### 7. Tax

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Gross receipts tax | Minn. Stat. §390.08 | 10% on retail sale price |

| Local taxes | Municipal authority | Municipalities may impose additional taxes within limits |

### 8. Employment

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Off-duty conduct protection | Minn. Stat. §181.938 | Employer may not discriminate for lawful off-duty cannabis use |
| Drug-free workplace policies | Employer authority | May maintain drug-free workplace; may test on reasonable suspicion, post-accident, random (with limitations) |
| Safety-sensitive carve-outs | Minn. Stat. §181.938 | DOT-regulated, security clearance, healthcare, federal contractor positions |
