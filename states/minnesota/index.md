    <h1>Minnesota</h1>
    <p class="summary">Adult-use established by the legislature via <strong>HF 100</strong> (2023) — not a ballot initiative — making it prone to gridlock and mid-course rulemaking. The Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) runs adult-use; the Department of Health runs medical under Ch. 152. Two parallel systems, separate METRC tracks. A 50%+ social-equity license reservation and an unregulated hemp-derived market shape the compliance environment.</p>
