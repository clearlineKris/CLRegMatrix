# Ohio Cannabis Regulatory Profile

## Overview

Ohio's adult-use cannabis market was created by voter-approved Issue 2 (November 2023) and is being actively reconstructed by a legislature that opposed it. The Division of Cannabis Control (DCC), housed under the Department of Commerce, stands up a dual-use apparatus under conditions of genuine inter-branch legal conflict. Adult-use possession became legal December 7, 2023; licensed retail launched August 2024 — leaving an eight-month gap where possession was legal but no legal purchase point existed. The compliance environment requires operators to track Issue 2 statute, legislative amendments, and DCC interpretations as three separate, sometimes contradictory layers.

## Legalization Status

| Dimension | Status | Effective Date |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Medical Cannabis | ✅ Legal (HB 523) | 2017 |
| Adult-Use Cannabis | ✅ Legal (Issue 2) | December 7, 2023 |
| Home Cultivation | 🟡 Legal with registration requirements | Legislative amendments pending operationalization |
| Retail Sales | ✅ Active (first licenses Aug 2024) | August 2024 |
| Hemp-Derived Cannabinoids | 🟡 Gray Zone | Pending rulemaking |
| Social Consumption | ❌ Not Legal | Not passed |

## Regulatory Structure

### Primary Agencies
| Agency | Role | Statute |
|--------|------|---------|
| Division of Cannabis Control (DCC) | Licensing, enforcement, rulemaking | ORC Chapter 3780 / 3796 |
| Ohio Dept of Commerce | DCC parent agency | — |
| Ohio Dept of Health | Medical cannabis program oversight | ORC Chapter 3796 |
| Ohio Legislature | Statute amendment, budget, policy | — |

### Legal Hierarchy
1. Ohio Constitution / Issue 2 (voter-approved)
2. Ohio Revised Code Chapters 3780 (adult-use) and 3796 (medical)
3. DCC Administrative Rules
4. Local ordinances (municipal opt-outs)

## Seed-to-Sale Tracking

- **System:** METRC (carried over from medical program)
- **Scope:** Plant tagging, harvest, packaging, transfer, sales
- **Key complexity:** Adult-use and medical inventory tracked in **separate METRC categories**. Transfer manifests must specify receiving license type. Product conversion between designations requires documented repackaging event with METRC entries.
- **Critical compliance note:** Medical-vs-adult-use category separation is the #1 DCC early inspection finding. Even product stored in the same facility must be in distinct METRC categories with license-type-specific transfer manifests.

## Key Statutes

- **ORC §3780.05** — Possession limits (2.5 oz flower / 15 g extract for adults)
- **ORC §3780.06** — Home cultivation (6 plants/person, 12/household — registration required per legislative amendments)
- **ORC Chapter 3796** — Medical cannabis program (continues parallel to adult-use)
- **DCC Rules** — Facility standards, security, inventory tracking, testing, advertising, disciplinary procedures

## Tax Architecture

| Tax | Rate | Applied To |
|-----|------|-----------|
| Excise Tax | 10% | Retail sale price |
| Sales Tax | Standard (7%+) | Pre-excise price |
| **Combined Consumer Rate** | ~17%+ | Varies by jurisdiction |

**Margin note:** Operators who model retail margin around the 10% excise figure alone are materially miscalculating their pricing floor.
