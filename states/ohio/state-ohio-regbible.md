# Ohio RegBible — Statutory Authority Reference

## Overview

Statutory authority for each compliance domain in Ohio. This document cites the codified law — the "letter of the law" that operators must comply with. Note: operators must also track legislative amendments and DCC interpretations as separate layers. Where those layers conflict, the most recent authoritative source governs.

---

## Compliance Domains

---

### Licensing

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Adult-use cannabis legalization | ORC §3780.01 et seq. | Defines legal framework for adult-use cannabis |
| Medical cannabis program | ORC Chapter 3796 | Continues parallel medical program |
| DCC rulemaking authority | ORC §3780.08 | DCC empowered to adopt rules implementing Chapter 3780 |
| Cultivation licenses | ORC §3780.15 | Level I and Level II cultivator licensing |
| Processor licenses | ORC §3780.17 | Processor licensing and operational standards |
| Dispensary licenses | ORC §3780.22 | Retail dispensary licensing |
| Testing lab licensing | ORC §3780.20 | Independent testing laboratory licensing |
| Dual-use conversion | DCC administrative rule | Streamlined conversion pathway for medical licensees |
| License renewal | ORC §3780.15 / DCC rules | Annual renewal requirements |
| License modification | DCC administrative rule | Change requests (ownership, location, etc.) |

### Operations

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Possession limits (adult) | ORC §3780.05 | 2.5 oz flower / 15 g extract; personal limits, not transaction caps |
| Possession limits (medical) | ORC §3796.10 | 90-day supply; patient and caregiver limits |
| Home cultivation (voter-approved) | ORC §3780.06 | 6 plants/person, 12/household |
| Home cultivation (legislative modifications) | SB 261 (amended) | Registration requirements, security mandates, proximity restrictions — enforcement mechanism unclear |
| Age restrictions | ORC §3780.05 | Adults 21+ for adult-use; qualifying patients for medical |
| Consumption on premises | ORC §3780.05 | Consumption on licensed premises prohibited unless authorized |
| Dual-use operations | DCC administrative rule | Separate tracking, separate inventory, separate manifests |

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|

| METRC: plant tagging | DCC Rules 4725 | RFID tags on all plants within 24 hours |
