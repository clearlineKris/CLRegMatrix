    <h1>Ohio</h1>
    <p class="summary">Adult-use created by voter-approved <strong>Issue 2</strong> (Nov 2023); retail launched Aug 2024. The Division of Cannabis Control (DCC), under the Department of Commerce, stands up a dual-use apparatus amid genuine inter-branch conflict — operators must track Issue 2, legislative amendments, and DCC interpretations as three sometimes-contradictory layers. Medical (HB 523 / Ch. 3796) runs parallel.</p>
