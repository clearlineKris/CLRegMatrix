## Policy Context (Summary)

Ohio's cannabis regulatory framework is fundamentally shaped by the conflict between voter mandate (Issue 2, 63% approval) and legislative opposition (Republican supermajority). Operators must track three simultaneously valid layers: the Issue 2 statute, the SB 261 legislative amendments, and DCC implementing interpretations — any of which can shift compliance posture monthly.

The enforcement environment is education-first for good-faith operators but zero-tolerance for specific violations from day one (minors/diversion/METRC failures). Compliance strategy in Ohio cannot be separated from political intelligence — the regulatory landscape shifts month to month.
