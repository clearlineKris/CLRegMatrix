## What the full RegMatrix offers Ohio

Ohio's Issue 2 (Nov 2023, 63% approval) created adult-use legalization but triggered active legislative opposition — Republican supermajority introduced SB 261 to gut home cultivation and raise taxes. The result: an eight-month gap between possession legality (Dec 2023) and retail launch (Aug 2024), and enforcement that's education-first for good-faith operators but zero-tolerance for specific violations from day one.

Key structural features:
- **DCC (Division of Cannabis Control)** under Department of Commerce — dual-use authority
- **Issue 2 vs. SB 261 tension** — three overlapping regulatory layers
- **METRC dual tracking** — medical and adult-use inventory must be separate categories
- **Home grow** — 6 plants/person, 12/household (partially operationalized as of 2025)
- **17%+ combined tax rate** (10% excise + standard sales tax)
